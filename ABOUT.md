# About

## AI Governance Resource Series

This repository publishes practical, vendor-neutral frameworks for the responsible governance and management of artificial intelligence.

The objective is to contribute openly to the evolving field of AI governance by providing frameworks that organizations can adapt to their own operating environments. Rather than focusing solely on compliance or technology controls, this series emphasizes management principles that enable organizations to adopt AI responsibly while maintaining accountability, transparency, and effective oversight.

The frameworks are intended for executives, technology leaders, governance professionals, enterprise architects, risk practitioners, and anyone responsible for designing or overseeing AI-enabled organizations.

---

## Project Philosophy

This repository is built on three core principles:

- **AI is a tool, but responsibility remains with people.**
- **Agentic AI creates a digital workforce that should be managed, not merely deployed.**
- **Effective AI governance combines technology governance with sound management principles.**

These principles form the foundation of every publication within the AI Governance Resource Series.

---

## Publications

### Volume 1
**Responsible AI Use Framework**

A practical framework that establishes foundational principles for the responsible use of artificial intelligence, emphasizing that accountability always remains with the individual or organization using AI.

### Volume 2
**Governing the Digital Workforce**

A management framework for agentic AI that introduces the concept of the digital workforce and proposes governance principles for supervising AI agents as operational actors working alongside human teams.

Additional volumes will be published as the field of AI governance continues to evolve.
