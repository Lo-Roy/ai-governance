# About

## AI Governance Resource Series

The AI Governance Resource Series is a collection of openly published, vendor-neutral frameworks that explore the governance and management of artificial intelligence in modern organizations.

The series is intended to provide practical guidance that organizations can adapt to their own operating environments. Rather than focusing solely on technology or regulatory compliance, these publications emphasize management principles, organizational accountability, and responsible AI adoption.

The frameworks are written for executives, technology leaders, enterprise architects, governance professionals, risk practitioners, and anyone responsible for designing, implementing, or overseeing AI-enabled organizations.

---

## Project Philosophy

This repository is built on three core principles:

- **Artificial intelligence augments the way people and organizations work.**
- **People remain accountable for decisions, actions, and outcomes, regardless of how AI is used.**
- **Responsible AI adaptation requires practical governance, sound management, and continuous organizational adaptation.**

These principles form the foundation of every publication in the AI Governance Resource Series.

---

## Publications

### Volume 1

**Responsible AI Use Framework**

A practical framework that establishes foundational principles for the responsible use of artificial intelligence, emphasizing that accountability always remains with the individual or organization using AI.

### Volume 2

**Governing the Digital Workforce**

A management framework for agentic AI that introduces the concept of the digital workforce and proposes governance principles for supervising AI agents working alongside human teams.

Additional volumes will be published as the field of AI governance continues to evolve.
