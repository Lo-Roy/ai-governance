# AI Governance Resources

A curated collection of freely available AI governance resources by Roy Lo.

This repository contains selected frameworks, reference models, and guidance intended to support responsible AI adoption across organizations. The resources are shared to contribute to the broader AI community by making practical governance knowledge more accessible.

The materials published here represent selected public resources and are not intended to be a comprehensive implementation toolkit.

---

## Purpose

Artificial intelligence is transforming how organizations operate, creating new opportunities alongside new governance responsibilities.

This repository aims to:

- Share practical AI governance resources with the public
- Promote responsible and trustworthy AI practices
- Encourage learning and informed discussion
- Support organizations beginning or strengthening their AI governance capabilities

---

## Resources

Publications in this repository may include:

- AI Ethics Framework
- AI Guardrails Framework
- Governance reference models
- White papers
- Discussion papers
- Educational resources

Additional resources may be added over time.

## Intended Audience

These materials are intended for:

- Business leaders
- AI governance professionals
- Technology leaders
- Risk and compliance teams
- AI practitioners
- Researchers
- Educators
- Students

---

## Intended Use

These resources are provided to support learning, discussion, and responsible AI adoption.

They are intended as reference materials and should be adapted to each organization's objectives, governance model, regulatory environment, and risk profile.

Nothing in this repository should be interpreted as legal, regulatory, audit, or compliance advice.
